How to play

You and your opponent start with 100 HP and a hand of 3 cards — Fire, Lightning, Water or Nature. Each turn you have 2 AP (your very first turn is 1 AP if you go first); Playing a card or Drawing a new one each costs 1 AP. Your hand can hold at most 5 cards at once.

The board starts as a 3x3 grid and grows to 5x5 the moment the 3x3 fills up. You have 45 seconds to act on your turn — if time runs out, your turn ends automatically and play passes to your opponent.

Reduce your opponent to 0 HP first to win the game.

Combo guide

Placing 3 cards in a row, column or diagonal forms a Combo if they share an owner, a type, or both:

Owner Combo: all 3 cards placed by the same player (any mix of types) — 15 damage.

Type Combo: all 3 cards the same type, placed by either player (mix of A/B shown here) — 5 damage, credited to whoever completed it.

  • Owner Combo (same player placed all 3, any mix of types) — 15 damage.
  • Type Combo (same card type, placed by either player) — 5 damage, credited to whoever completed it.
  • Both at once still counts once, for 15 — never 15+5.

A Type Combo — same card type — also triggers that type's own effect:

  • Fire — 10 extra damage on top of the Combo's own damage.
  • Lightning — reduces the opponent's AP on their very next turn.
  • Water — draws 2 cards (fewer if your hand is close to its 5-card limit).
  • Nature — grants 1 extra AP, usable immediately this same turn.

A full board with neither player dead locks the game as a draw — it counts toward neither player's score.
